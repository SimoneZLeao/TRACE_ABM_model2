TRACE v2

Trajectory Reconstruction through Agent-based Cultural Emergence

TRACE is an agent-based model for investigating artistic career trajectories through interactions between work, time, economic survival, symbolic capital, and artistic self-realisation. Version 2 models how artists navigate artistic practice and employment opportunities over time.
